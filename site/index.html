What We Do

End-to-end CRM data quality and enrichment

We don't just sell you a tool. We own the entire data lifecycle so your team just sells.

1

Data Audit & Health Report

We profile every field in your CRM — duplicates, missing values, stale records, format inconsistencies. You get a scored report showing exactly where the leaks are and what they cost you.

2

Cleanse & Deduplicate

We fix phone formats, merge duplicate contacts and accounts, remove dead records, and standardize every field. Your CRM becomes a single source of truth your team actually trusts.

3

Enrich & Normalize

We fill in missing emails, direct dials, job titles, company data, and firmographics using AI and best-in-class data providers. Job titles are standardized. Industries are normalized. Every record becomes a full profile.

4

AI Lead Scoring & Segmentation

We build scoring models based on your ICP — completeness, recency, seniority, industry fit — so reps know who to call first. Marketing gets clean segments. Leadership gets accurate forecasts.
