### Key Insight: Tools vs. Service
All these tools have the same problem — they enrich **incoming** data but don't:
- Clean what's already there
- Deduplicate existing records
- Normalize messy historical data
- Build scoring models specific to the company's ICP
- Monitor and maintain quality over time
- Train reps on data hygiene

**That gap between "we have a tool" and "our data actually works" is your entire business.**
