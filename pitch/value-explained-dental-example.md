### What You Do (The Service)

**Week 1: Audit**
You get an export from their practice management software (CSV or Excel).
You run your audit script. In 10 minutes, you hand them this:

> "Dr. Smith, here's what I found in your patient database:
> - 3,000 total records
> - 207 duplicates (same patient listed multiple times)
> - 20% of records have no phone number
> - 30% have no email address
> - 800 patients haven't been in for 12+ months
> - Your data health score is 62 out of 100 — Grade D
>
> That means roughly $200,000 in annual revenue is walking out the door
> because your team can't reach the right patients at the right time."

They've never seen this. Nobody has ever shown them a number.
This is the moment they realize the problem is real.

**Week 2: Clean**
You run your cleansing scripts:
- Merge 207 duplicates into single records (keeping the best info from each)
- Standardize all phone formats: (555) 123-4567
- Fix invalid emails, flag personal vs. work emails
- Segment patients into categories:
  - Active (visited in last 6 months)
  - Overdue (6-12 months since last visit)
  - Inactive (12-18 months)
  - Lost (18+ months)
  - New (first visit in last 90 days)

**Week 3: Enrich**
For patients missing contact info, you use enrichment APIs to find:
- Updated phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Whether they've moved (address validation)

You turn 600 unreachable patients into 400+ reachable patients.

**Week 4: Score and Deliver**
You score every patient:
- **Hot (reactivate now):** Overdue 6-12 months, has phone + email, previously high-value (crowns, implants, etc.)
- **Warm (nurture):** Inactive 12-18 months, has at least one contact method
- **Cold (long shot):** Lost 18+ months, limited contact info
- **Priority new:** Recent first visit, hasn't booked follow-up yet

You hand Dr. Smith:
1. **A cleaned database** — 2,793 unique records (207 dupes removed), properly segmented
2. **A "Call These First" list** — 150 high-value patients who are overdue and reachable
3. **A "Reactivation Campaign" list** — 400 inactive patients with verified contact info, ready for text/email
4. **A health report** showing before/after: Score went from 62 to 89
