Dental & Medical Practices

The average practice has 30-40% of patients overdue. 20% have no phone number on file. 30% have no email. That's thousands of dollars walking away because your team can't reach them.

  • Merge duplicate patient records
  • Find missing phone numbers and emails
  • Segment: Active, Overdue, Inactive, Lost
  • Deliver a reactivation list ready for text/email campaign

HVAC, Plumbing & Electrical

Years of service calls, never cleaned. Same address listed multiple times. No segmentation between commercial and residential. Can't identify maintenance agreement upsell opportunities.

  • Clean years of service records
  • Segment: commercial vs residential, service agreement vs one-time
  • Find customers due for seasonal maintenance
  • Map customer density by zip code for route optimization
Typical result: $15,000+ in maintenance agreement upsells identified

Staffing & Recruiting Agencies

30% of candidate records missing contact details. Duplicate candidates everywhere. Recruiters source externally because they can't find qualified candidates already in the database.

  • Deduplicate candidate records
  • Standardize skills, titles, and trade categories
  • Enrich missing phone, email, LinkedIn
  • Segment by skill, availability, location
Typical result: Fill roles from existing database — save $5,000+ per placement in sourcing

Manufacturers

CRM disconnected from ERP. Reps promise dates they can't keep. 17,000 SKUs in Excel. Contact data for distributors, dealers, and customers all mixed together with no segmentation.

  • Clean and deduplicate customer/distributor records
  • Normalize product categories, territories, account types
  • Enrich missing buyer contacts (purchasing managers, engineers)
  • Build lead scoring so reps call the right accounts first
Typical result: Reps save 13 hrs/week hunting for info — close more deals

Wholesale Distributors

62% report significant CRM data gaps. 55% can't identify at-risk customers. Managing thousands of SKUs with complex pricing across ship-to/bill-to accounts.

  • Deduplicate ship-to/bill-to accounts
  • Normalize customer segments and product categories
  • Build churn risk scoring — flag declining accounts
  • Deliver "Top 50 At-Risk Accounts" report
